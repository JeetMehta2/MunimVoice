MERCHANT REALITY & PAIN POINTS
The Small Merchant Dilemma: High Volume, Zero Visibility
Ramesh (Chai Stall, Jaipur) processes 150+ UPI payments daily, yet operates completely blind.
1. Invisible Margin Leak
- UPI captures top-line revenue, but cash collections and daily vendor expenses go unrecorded.
- True daily net profit remains unknown until bank balance runs short.
- Illustrative: ~15% estimated monthly profit leak from unrecorded informal cash costs.
2. Unrecovered Customer Udhaar
- Informal customer credits accumulate on scattered paper slips without structured tracking.
- Merchants avoid awkward manual collection calls with familiar regular customers.
- Illustrative: ~Rs 4,200 average monthly working capital frozen in unpaid udhaar.
3. Reactive Operations
- Without POS barcode systems, merchants cannot identify top-selling item combos.
- Zero advance preparation for predictable rush surges and afternoon quiet lulls.
- Marketing and combo discounts are never run due to setup friction and lack of time.
The Fundamental Gap
- Existing digital ledger apps only record transactions. Munim actively understands, recommends, and acts.

--- SLIDE 5 ---
CORE AI INNOVATION
Item Inference: Menu Discovery from UPI Streams
Reconstructing catalog and combo baskets from anonymous payment frequencies without POS hardware.
Evaluation Benchmarks on Synthetic Data
- Easy Baseline (4 distinct price points): Precision 1.0, Recall 1.0, F1 1.0.
- Hard Stress Test (7 items, 2 price collisions, combos, noise, sparse item): Precision 0.50, Recall 0.71, F1 0.59, Top-1 Accuracy 0.57.
3-Stage Inference Methodology
- 1. Price-Point Clustering: Extracts modal transaction amounts from raw stream.
- 2. Domain Priors: Maps clusters to trade items and 2-item combo equations.
- 3. Bayesian Confirmation: Merchant confirms candidate hypotheses via UI chips.
Practical Merchant Benefit
- Delivers enterprise-grade SKU and basket analysis to street stalls without requiring barcode scanners.

FINANCIAL INCLUSION & LENDING SIGNAL
Munim Score: Behavioral Underwriting Enrichment
A real-time operational discipline score designed to enrich Paytm's licensed lending underwriting.
5 Behavioral Health Dimensions (0-100)
- 1. Volume Consistency: Daily transaction stability across weeks.
- 2. Expense Discipline: Daily logging of cash outflows and supplier costs.
- 3. Repayment Regularity: Velocity of supplier and vendor settlements.
- 4. Udhaar Health: Ratio of active customer credits to recovered dues.
- 5. Operational Stability: Regular trading hours and business consistency.
Enrichment Signal, Not Credit Decision
- A behavioral signal that could enrich Paytm's existing, licensed underwriting.
- Not a formal credit decision or loan offer; contains no pre-approved limits.
In-App Disclaimer Enforced
- Visible in mobile UI: 'Munim Score is a behavioral management indicator and does not constitute a formal loan offer or statutory credit rating.'
