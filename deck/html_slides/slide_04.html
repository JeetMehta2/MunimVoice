02
Critical Field Challenges
Street Noise & Chatter Acoustic Limit
High ambient street and stall chatter makes continuous on-device mic wake-word listening unreliable.
Unrecorded Cash & Expenses Data Blindspot
UPI records digital revenue, but informal vendor cash payouts (milk, gas) remain completely off-book.
Price-Point Collisions Inference Limit
Multiple distinct items sharing the same price (e.g. ₹10 Chai vs ₹10 Parle-G Biscuit) confuse raw clustering.
LLM Arithmetic Hallucinations Trust Hazard
General-purpose LLMs hallucinate calculations, percentages, and cash balances when doing inline math.
Social Awkwardness with Debtors Behavioral Trap
Merchants hesitate to manually call familiar neighbors for overdue udhaar due to social discomfort.
03
Tested Mitigations
Output-Only Soundbox Architecture Hardware Safe
Soundbox acts strictly as an audio speaker directing to phone; speech/typing input is isolated to mobile.
30-Second Bahi-Khata Ritual Zero Friction
Quick evening spoken/typed Hindi ritual reconciles cash expenses instantly with UPI for True Daily Profit.
Combinatorial Priors + Confirm Chips Human-in-Loop
Maps price clusters to ranked trade priors; merchant confirms candidate hypotheses with a single tap.
Zero Arithmetic in LLM 0 Math Errors
LLM is restricted to intent classification; 100% of profit, margin, and scores computed deterministically in Python.
Tone-Controlled WhatsApp Drafts Respectful
Merchants select Polite, Formal, or Firm reminder tones; links dispatched only upon explicit approval.
